## Best selling channel

### Facebook Marketplace
Best first choice for ordinary laptops, desktops, iMacs and bulky systems in Dubbo. Local pickup removes shipping cost and risk, and buyers can see the machine working.

### Gumtree
Use as a duplicate local/regional listing, especially for desktops, iMacs and lower-value computers where national postage makes little sense.

### eBay Australia
Most useful for hardware with a national enthusiast audience:
- ASUS ROG Crosshair V Formula-Z motherboard / parts
- XFX Radeon R9 290
- ThinkPad T61
- MacBook Air Early 2015
- unusual or collectible parts

## Practical interpretation

If the goal is maximum cash rather than speed, list the best machines individually, use strong photos, show SSD/battery/thermal health where possible, and wait for the right buyer.

If the goal is to clear the fleet within roughly a month, a **$2,700–$3,200 total outcome** is more realistic than holding every machine at its theoretical maximum.

The biggest-value systems deserve individual listings. The weakest older machines may be more efficient to bundle, sell cheaply to local buyers, or use as upgrade/repair stock rather than spending hours chasing an extra $20.
