DUBBO REFURBISHED LAPTOP MARKET

Could refurbishing laptops actually make money?

Current evidence says there is a plausible local market, but the opportunity depends much more on buying well than on simply adding RAM or an SSD.

Market signal

There is already local demand.

Leading Edge Computers Dubbo explicitly advertises refurbished computers, useful evidence that the category already exists locally rather than being purely an online niche.

Best resale lane

Think $150–$250, not ancient $50 laptops.

The strongest target is a clean business-class Latitude, EliteBook/ProBook or ThinkPad with Windows 11-supported hardware, 8 GB RAM, SSD, healthy battery and charger.

Buying rule

Demand about $80–$100 visible gross margin.

Hidden faults, batteries, chargers, parts, negotiation, returns and your time can eat a thin margin very quickly.

WHY DUBBO CAN SUPPORT IT

Enough people, families and price-conscious buyers

The 2021 Census recorded 54,922 people in Dubbo Regional, median age 36, and median weekly household income of $1,597.

Dubbo locality itself recorded 43,516 people, median age 35, with household income below the NSW median. That does not prove sales volume, but it supports a meaningful budget-computing customer base.

2026 HARDWARE REALITY

Windows 10 changed the value of old stock

Windows 10 support ended on 14 October 2025. For normal resale stock, Windows 11-supported machines are therefore easier to position responsibly than older unsupported hardware.

Preferred normal floor: Intel 8th gen or newer, or comparable officially supported Ryzen hardware. Older 6th/7th-gen systems can still work when acquired very cheaply.

IMPORTANT NSW COMPLIANCE

A proper flipping business has real compliance costs.

NSW treats electronic goods as prescribed second-hand goods. A business buying and reselling them generally needs a second-hand dealer licence. The current 2026–27 fee for a new 1-year licence is $692.

Current guidance also generally requires supplier ID/ownership records, electronic transaction records, acquisition reporting to NSW Police within three days, and a 14-day hold period before regulated goods are altered or resold.

BEST NEXT TEST

Test one real Dubbo listing before buying bulk stock.

Take one genuinely surplus laptop you already own, clean and test it properly, publish battery/SSD health and a clear condition description, then measure views, messages, offers and time-to-sale for 7–14 days. That gives better evidence than guessing from asking prices.

THE RULE

$100 means $100 all-in.

A “$70 computer” with $40 postage is not a $70 bargain. For complete PCs and laptops, this board counts item price + shown delivery + mandatory missing parts. Unknown-shipping systems are kept off the qualifying board.

Parts are listed separately because a $78 GPU is useful market intelligence even when it cannot make a complete $100 build by itself. Compatibility is not assumed.
