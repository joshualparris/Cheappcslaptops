What did the full $100 sweep conclude?

The strongest fixed-price result was a $99 4th-gen ex-office desktop. The HP ProDesk 600 G1 is the better ready-to-use pick because it includes an SSD; the $99 Dell OptiPlex 9020 has the stronger i7-4770 CPU but an HDD.

No complete dedicated-GPU gaming PC at or below $100 all-in was verified in the public-web sweep. A 7th-gen OptiPlex 7050 Micro auction was under the cap when observed, but auctions are shown as watch items, never guaranteed final prices.

THE RULE

$100 means $100 all-in.

A “$70 computer” with $40 postage is not a $70 bargain. For complete PCs and laptops, this board counts item price + shown delivery + mandatory missing parts. Unknown-shipping systems are kept off the qualifying board.

Parts are listed separately because a $78 GPU is useful market intelligence even when it cannot make a complete $100 build by itself. Compatibility is not assumed.
