# Deep Research Prompt — $100 PC Challenge

Use this prompt for a fresh, current market sweep.

---

I want you to conduct a **deep, current Australian marketplace search** to answer:

> **How powerful a complete usable PC can we obtain for a HARD MAXIMUM OF $100 AUD, including shipping to Dubbo NSW 2830 or realistic local pickup?**

This is a real buying challenge, not a theoretical parts list.

Do not rely on only one marketplace or one search engine.

## Hard budget rule

The final total must be **$100 AUD or less**.

Include:

- item price
- postage/freight
- mandatory fees
- essential adapters
- any missing RAM, storage, PSU or GPU needed to make the proposed machine usable

Do not describe something as a $100 build if it exceeds $100 after delivery or required parts.

If shipping cannot be verified, label it clearly as **shipping unverified** and do not rank it above a fully verified option purely on price.

For pickup listings, state the town and whether the trip is realistically worthwhile from Dubbo.

## What counts as complete

The challenge is for the tower/system itself.

Already available:
- monitor
- keyboard
- mouse
- network

Windows activation is not required. Linux is acceptable.

A machine missing storage/RAM/PSU/etc. only qualifies if the required parts can be added while keeping the all-in total at or below $100.

Search independently for:

Then test combinations against the hard $100 total.

### GPUs worth watching

Examples:
- GTX 750 Ti / 950 / 960 / 1050 / 1050 Ti
- GTX 660 / 670 / 760
- RX 460 / 550 / 560
- HD 7850 / 7870
- R9 270 / 270X / 280
- overlooked Quadro / FirePro / OEM cards with useful gaming capability

Do not assume compatibility. Check:
- full-height vs low-profile
- slot width
- card length
- PSU wattage
- PCIe power connectors
- proprietary PSU/motherboard limits

## Reject common traps

Be suspicious of:
- GT 710 / weak GT 730 DDR3 machines marketed as gaming PCs
- fake/mislabelled GPUs
- ancient CPUs with inflated “gaming” descriptions
- SFF systems paired with full-height GPUs
- untested systems unless repair risk is priced appropriately
- missing coolers/PSUs/drives
- proprietary systems needing expensive adapters
- listings whose postage makes the deal pointless
- listings that are actually unavailable/out of stock

Open listings and verify availability where practical.
