### Assistant

Yes — **pretty much all the pre-2004 games on the supplied list should run on all four laptops from a hardware point of view**.

The assistant further clarified:

- 1994–1999 games: effectively yes across the board.
- 2000–2003 games: still generally yes, but the Lenovo J1800 and Acer N4500 4 GB may need low settings or lower resolution on heavier 3D titles.
- The Lenovo's HDD may also make loading and general responsiveness slower.
- For many older games, operating-system compatibility is more likely to be the obstacle than raw performance.
